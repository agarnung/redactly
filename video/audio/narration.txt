Every screenshot AI tool asks you to upload your whole screen. But screenshots are full of API keys, customer emails, IBANs and tokens. This one does the opposite.
A developer debugging a 401. A support agent with a customer ticket. A bank statement. Three very common screenshots, all full of secrets.
Gemini Nano reads the image locally. It finds emails, API keys, tokens, IBANs, card numbers, addresses and names, replaces them with placeholders, and paints black boxes over the pixels. The labels show only the type, never the value.
The model can miss something. I can drag a manual box, or click one to remove it. The redacted image, the receipt and the risk card update immediately.
A short question stays on-device. No raw image sent. A complex question goes to the cloud with only the sanitized payload, streamed back in seconds. That is real hybrid inference, with a privacy guard.
The cloud only gets what I explicitly choose: text, redacted image, or both. The risk card shows zero secrets sent. The receipt hashes the sanitized text and the redacted image, so the claim is auditable.
Detectors, Luhn, box normalization, leak check, routing policy and receipt hashing are covered by Node tests. The on-device model uses structured JSON output through Firebase AI Logic; the cloud leg is a separate, explicit step.
Redact locally, ask globally. The code is open source under GPL-3.0. Thanks for watching.
